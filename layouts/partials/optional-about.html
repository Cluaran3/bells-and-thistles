Hi, I'm Nadya — a long-time journalist turned technical writer. Throughout my professional career I practice more or less the same thing: I talk to people, figure out what's missing, analyze and structure information to communicate it to other people in a clear and concise way.

My current tools include Markdown, pull requests, static site generators, Swagger, AI agents, and CI-triggered publishing pipelines — instead of a voice recorder, pen, and paper notebook. Nevertheless, I still enjoy writing texts and occasionally indulge myself with using an em-dash or two.

Besides my work, I'm a motorcycle girl, a gym rat, an amateur karting racer, once in a blue moon a home baker, and a mother to one teenager, one toddler, and one middle-aged Belgian Malinois.

And yes, I like thistles, obviously. They are my mascot from a young age: first as a symbol of all-things-Scottish (I actually wrote my diploma on Scottish national identity in the 19th century and even ran a school of Scottish dancing for some time), later on — as a representation of resilience and adaptability.

I'm currently based in Belgrade, Serbia, where I've spent the last four years discovering the language, the culture, and the natural beauty of my new home.
